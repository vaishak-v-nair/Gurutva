Four different arrangements of buried rock, differing by more than the whole body anyone is looking for. Yet they predict readings that sit within a whisker of each other. Ordinary software picks one of them and draws it for you, in colour, with no warning.

All four sets of readings, on top of each other. They differ by at most 0.111 — about twice the wobble of the instrument — while the pictures above differ by 0.53 g/cc, more than the entire body being hunted. Tiny differences up here, enormous differences down there. That is what makes this hard, and it does not go away by buying a better meter.

So which one is true? Nobody knows. That is the whole problem, and almost nothing in this industry admits it.

Three things it is allowed to say

NOT CLAIMED

A check failed. The numbers are diagnostics. Do not put them in a decision.

PROVISIONAL

Every check passed, but nothing was compared against a known right answer — because real data has none.

CLAIMABLE

Every check passed and it was verified against a known answer. Rare, and meant to be.

How you actually use it

  1. Download the zip and unpack it anywhere.
  2. Make sure you have Python 3.10 or newer, then once: pip install numpy scipy matplotlib
  3. Double-click Gurutva.bat on Windows, or run ./gurutva on Mac and Linux. A window opens.
  4. Press Use the example, then Run, to watch it work on a survey that ships with it.
  5. Then choose your own CSV and answer three questions about your site. We do not guess them for you: they are statements about your ground, not ours.
