The hole this run found in our own gate suite

gates passed4 of 4
aperture mass vs known truthmisses by 11.6 sigma — interval does not cover
true peak against the declared prior0.597 is a 4.0-sigma excursion under a prior of 0.15
what each gate was actually testinglicensing: is the DATA plausible. calibration: is the posterior self-consistent for truths drawn FROM the prior. stability: do two runs agree. adequacy: does the answer reproduce the data. None asks whether a REAL truth lands inside the interval
This is the same species of blind spot that created gate 4, found the same way — by checking against a truth we happened to know. The exclusion limit survives it because shrinkage makes an upper bound conservative. A mass estimate does not, so it is not claimed here. Open question, deliberately left open: whether the suite needs a fifth gate for coverage at a fixed realistic truth, or whether the prior class must be declared wide enough to contain the object being searched for.
